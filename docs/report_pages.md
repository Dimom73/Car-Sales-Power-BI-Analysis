# Report Pages

The Power BI report includes five main pages.

## 1. Sales Overview

Purpose:

- show overall sales performance
- track sales over time
- compare total sales and sales count
- provide high-level KPI overview

Main visuals:

- KPI cards
- line chart by date hierarchy
- combo chart for sales count and total sales by month
- slicers by year, quarter, month, and dealer region
- forecast visual

## 2. Company Analysis

Purpose:

- compare car companies by sales performance
- analyze total sales, sales count, average price, and sales share

Main visuals:

- treemap by total sales
- clustered column chart by sales count
- clustered column chart by average price
- slicers by date and company

## 3. Car Characteristics Analysis

Purpose:

- analyze product-side sales patterns
- understand which models and car characteristics are most common

Main visuals:

- top models by total sales
- sales count by body style
- sales count by engine
- sales count by transmission
- sales count by color

## 4. Customer Analysis

Purpose:

- analyze sales from the customer perspective
- compare customer groups by gender and income

Main visuals:

- average annual income card
- sales count card
- funnel chart by income group
- donut chart by gender
- average price by gender

## 5. Dealer & Region Analysis

Purpose:

- compare dealer regions and individual dealers
- identify stronger and weaker dealer performance

Main visuals:

- total sales by dealer region
- total sales by dealer name
- matrix with dealer performance measures
- slicers by date and dealer region

## Advanced Analytics

A forecast was added to the Sales Overview page.

Forecast setup:

- value: Total Sales
- date field: Month Start
- forecast length: 3 months
- seasonality: Auto
- confidence interval: 95%
